Running

Make no mistake — I run for leisure and for fun, not to run fast, far, or hard.

I started running as a little boy: my mother would let me run in the school yard early in the morning. My alma mater, Tsinghua University, requires all male undergraduates to finish 3 km (1,500 m for women) in a little over fifteen minutes — mandatory if you want to be eligible for a Bachelor’s degree.

I ran my first 5k in 2001, my first 10k in 2002, and my first half marathon (2:28) in 2003, all at the Beijing Marathon. In January 2026 I ran the Hong Kong Half Marathon in 2:23 with my friend Tirupam — a course of ups, downs, and a two-kilometre tunnel under Victoria Harbour.

Over the years I have found running to be a great way of exploring cities and neighbourhoods. The streets, the architecture, the nature, the culture and the people become familiar over the course of about ten kilometres.
